Data deserves the custody standards of money

Attestra exists because valuable datasets now spend their lives in infrastructure their owners don't control — and increasingly, they change hands. We build the verification layer that makes both facts safe.

Trust is a measurement, not a feeling

Storage contracts promise durability; only verification demonstrates it. Ownership records assert custody; only cryptography enforces it. Everything we ship converts an assumption into a check that either passes or fails — visibly, repeatably, in front of the customer.

Principle 01 Claims ship as code

Every security property has an executable validation in the product. If we can't demonstrate it live, we don't claim it.

Principle 02 Numbers are measured

Performance figures come from the deployment you're using — charted at view time, never pasted from a datasheet.

Principle 03 Custody leaves a trail

Every protection, verification, membership change and transfer is logged with its outcome. Evidence, not anecdotes.

Research foundation

The Attestra verification engine implements the group-transaction auditing scheme of Wu, You & Huang, published in the IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (vol. 20, 2025) — independently implemented from the published design, ported to the modern BLS12-381 curve, and validated end to end on commodity hardware.

Independent implementation Written from the published specification, with the verification equation re-derived before a line of code, then pinned by a 41-case suite.
Original findings Implementation surfaced two notational inconsistencies in the published scheme and showed its detection bound is conservative in every measured configuration.
Reproduced results The published performance claims — including the headline scaling advantage — reproduce on commodity hardware, with the data to show it.

An engineering-led team

Attestra is developed as the Major Project of a four-member engineering team — Sanjay G P, Shashank Gowda T K, Srujangouda Malipatil and Yashwanth Gowda D K — in the Department of Information Science & Engineering, Global Academy of Technology. The platform doubles as the team's demonstration environment: everything on this site runs the real protocol, live.
