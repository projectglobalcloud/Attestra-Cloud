Common questions

Why does a dataset show Needs re-protection? Protection keys live in secure memory only; a service restart rotates them out. Protect the dataset again from Storage.
Why did a verification fail? Either the storage layer no longer holds the exact data, or the verifying party's credentials were revoked by a transfer.
Does a transfer move my data? No — blocks are re-sealed in place with a fixed-size update.
How do schedules affect my bill? Scheduled verifications count toward the same monthly allowance as manual ones.
